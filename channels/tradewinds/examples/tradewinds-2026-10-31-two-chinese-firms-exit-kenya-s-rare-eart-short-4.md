# Follow one magnet in your own home

Pick one gadget in your home and follow its magnet. Your phone buzzes because of a tiny motor with a rare earth magnet inside. Big buyers are now following that same trail, and hedging at every step. Reuters reported in October that TSMC, the world's largest contract chipmaker, has surveyed suppliers about rare-earth-free motors for its factory equipment, according to three industry executives. In Birmingham, a plant run by HyProMag turns scrapped magnets back into new ones, skipping the mine and the refinery entirely. In the United States, recyclers are pulling magnets out of retired wind turbines. Each move is a bet that the chain can be shortened. You can do the cheap version of this exercise in five minutes. Choose one product, trace it to its chokepoint, and notice how often the same few steps appear. Which product did you pick?
