# A Gas Exporter Now Buys American Gas

One of the world's biggest gas exporters is now buying gas from America. QatarEnergy remains under force majeure, a legal notice that it cannot deliver, because the Strait of Hormuz is largely blocked. According to The National, it has signed a deal with Woodside for American LNG, and the extra volumes make it the largest buyer of US LNG. Consider what that means. To honour contracts with its own customers, an exporter is paying for fuel to cross an ocean from the other side of the world. Every extra mile adds freight, insurance and time. That cost does not disappear. It is spread across utilities in Europe and Asia, and eventually across household bills. Trade did not stop. It took a longer route and sent the invoice down the line. Who do you think ends up paying most?
