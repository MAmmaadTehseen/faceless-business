Narrate like a cinematic recap, not an explainer. Open with a one- or two-sentence hook that drops the viewer into the most gripping moment and poses the question the story answers. Tell the story in order, in present tense, with short punchy sentences and rising tension; notice in the moment what the camera, cutting and performances are doing. Close with a short honest verdict: what works, what does not, who should watch. Each Short opens with its own hook on a different beat and stands alone. Signal spoilers. Narration is original; do not reproduce dialogue at length. Energetic, confident delivery.
