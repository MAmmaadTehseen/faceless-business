# The FTC Is Asking What Drivers Get Paid

The FTC is not just asking what you pay. It is asking what drivers get paid. This week the agency ordered Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Instacart and others to hand over how they set prices and pay. In its announcement, the FTC said ride-share and delivery firms may make shifting pay offers based on opaque formulas. It even named possible inputs, like phone usage and a driver's history of accepting past offers. Think about what that could mean. If a system learns you accept low offers, it may keep sending low offers. That is a reasonable guess, not a proven fact. Proving or disproving it is exactly what this study is for. The companies have forty-five days to answer. If you drive or deliver, have you noticed offers change after you turn a few down?
