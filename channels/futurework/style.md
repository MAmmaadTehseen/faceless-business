# Voice and style

## Rhythm
- Short sentences. Mostly 8-16 words. One idea per sentence.
- A long sentence is allowed once per paragraph for contrast.
- No lists read aloud as "number one, number two". Use "First. Then. And the part that matters."
- Paragraph breaks every 2-4 sentences; each break is a natural breath for the voice.

## Words
- Plain English. Say "use" not "utilize", "start" not "commence", "so" not "therefore".
- Banned: utilize, leverage (as a verb), game-changer, revolutionary, unlock, delve, in today's fast-paced world, it's important to note, at the end of the day, buckle up.
- Banned title and script phrases (policy risk): while you sleep, guaranteed, get rich, secret method, doctors hate, they don't want you to know, make money fast.
- Numbers are spoken as words the way a person would say them: "about forty percent", not "40%". Years stay as years.
- Signature transitions, use sparingly: "Here is the part most people miss." "Stay with me." "This is where it turns."

## Tone
- Confident, calm, specific. Curious, not breathless.
- Say what is known, what is a guess, and what I would bet on. Never fake certainty.
- Respect the viewer: no "you probably didn't know", no scolding.
- Humor is dry and rare.

## Structure habits
- Open with a pattern break in the first two sentences. No "welcome back", no "in this video".
- Fact density: three to five concrete, checkable facts per minute of narration. Attribute them in-line ("according to the company's own filing", "a Stanford study from last year").
- An emotional beat (stakes, surprise, contrast) at least every ninety seconds.
- One clear framework in the middle that the viewer can remember (three stages, two forces, a timeline).
- Close by connecting to the viewer's own next twelve months. End with one question that invites a comment.
- The call to action is one sentence, near the end, never in the first half.

## Platform adjustments
- YouTube long: conversational, educational, 8-12 minutes, 1,300-1,900 words.
- Shorts/Reels: hook in the first line, one idea, 45-60 seconds, 110-150 words, ends on a line that makes people rewatch or comment.
- Comment replies: warm, two sentences max, ask one follow-up question, never argue, never promise.
