# Milk in Mandarin: niúnǎi

How do you say milk in Mandarin?
Mandarin. Milk is niúnǎi, rising niú, dipping nǎi.
Picture a brand new cow, niú, filling your glass with milk, nǎi. One tip: in Taiwan, cartons of fresh milk often say xiānrǔ instead, so you already know two words.
