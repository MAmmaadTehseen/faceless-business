# $20 Billion Isn't the Whole Moon Base Bill

Twenty billion dollars sounds like the price of a Moon base. It isn't the whole bill. In March, NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman said the agency would invest about twenty billion over seven years, and he was clear that this covers only the first two phases. In June, CNN reported that phase one alone runs to about ten billion dollars through 2028, and described the complete base at around thirty billion. Isaacman says the money comes from a ten billion dollar boost in last year's budget reconciliation law, plus regular yearly funding. NASA's bet is that pausing the Gateway lunar station and buying commercial rides stretches every dollar further. That's a plan, not a promise, and Congress still writes the checks each year. So here's the real question: is twenty billion a budget, or an opening bid?
