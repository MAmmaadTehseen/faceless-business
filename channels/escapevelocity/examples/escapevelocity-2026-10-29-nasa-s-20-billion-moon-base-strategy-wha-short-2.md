# The Artemis Crew That Won't Leave Earth Orbit

Four astronauts are set to fly an Artemis mission next year and never leave Earth orbit. That's Artemis III now. NASA turned it from a Moon landing into a test flight, and at its October 23rd briefing described fourteen to seventeen days in low Earth orbit, targeted for summer 2027. The crew in Orion will dock with test versions of both commercial Moon landers, SpaceX's Starship and Blue Origin's Blue Moon. Why stay close to home? If a docking goes wrong a few hundred kilometres up, the way home is much shorter than from lunar orbit. Every latch, hatch and handshake tested here is one less unknown on the real thing. The landing itself moves to Artemis IV, targeted for early 2028. Would you volunteer for the test flight, or wait for the landing?
