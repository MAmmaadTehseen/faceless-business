# Plainsight Media Article Rules

Write educational explainers derived from the channel's video script, in its distinct persona. Stay tightly on the exact topic. Do not invent facts, quotes, capabilities or sources. No local-business framing, services, location insertion, or staged approval workflow.

- Use 4-6 main sections, each with a genuinely different angle, mechanism, comparison, limitation or decision insight. No conceptual repetition or headings invented for SEO. Use Title Case headings and at most two H3s per section. FAQ questions use sentence case.
- Write simple, natural, specific human English. Avoid filler, AI phrasing, generic openers and transitions, exaggerated claims and repetitive conclusions. No em or en dashes anywhere. Use paragraphs primarily, bullets sparingly. At most one useful table, with a one-line introduction before and a takeaway after it.
- Start with a 90-110 word introduction stating why this exact topic matters and what the article covers. Then a 30-40 word Quick Answer and 4-5 concise Key Takeaways containing only facts explained later.
- Choose the topic's main search phrase as primary_keyword. Use that exact phrase naturally once in the body around word 500 (roughly words 450-600), supported by the section's explanation. Never put it in the introduction or any heading, or save it for the ending or the section immediately before the CTA.
- Hedge uncertain causes with can, may, can contribute to, or may indicate. Distinguish evidence from inference. No medical, legal, financial or income claims or advice, promises or guarantees. Attribute technical claims and statistics to verified sources.
- Integrate 3-4 evidence boxes, each from a different authoritative non-competitor organization AND registered domain: government, university, research institution, professional body or official statistics. Search for real source URLs and verify their relevance. Labels: Fact, Important, Do You Know, Stat, Pro Tip. Each adds a new mechanism, statistic, limitation or decision insight rather than repeating the section. Place each after its section's first paragraph, followed by continuation text. Include source_url and source_name.
- After the main sections and before FAQs, provide two short CTA paragraphs. Begin with a topic-relevant invitation, link the channel's YouTube page, invite the reader to watch the embedded video and subscribe. No client-service promotion.
- Exactly five FAQs, each answer 40-60 words. Add practical concerns, edge cases, limitations or misconceptions not fully explained in the main sections. Do not restate H2s. No accordion instructions.
- Meta title: at most 65 characters, distinct from (not almost identical to) H1. Meta description: at most 165 characters, accurate and natural. List only NLP entities actually used in the article.

## Final QA Checklist
Complete self_qa after all fields are written, marking each item PASS or FAIL and correcting failures: exact topic focus; distinct section purposes; no conceptual overlap or SEO-only headings; 4-6 sections and at most two H3s each; heading capitalization and sentence-case FAQ questions; natural specific English without filler; intro and Quick Answer lengths; 4-5 supported takeaways; exact keyword once around word 500 outside intro/headings/ending; contextual keyword placement; accurate attributed claims and hedged uncertainty; no prohibited claims; paragraphs primary and bullets sparse; at most one table with introduction and takeaway; five new-value FAQs with 40-60 words each; 3-4 authoritative evidence boxes with distinct organizations and registered domains; real relevant URLs; evidence adds new information and follows first paragraphs; two-paragraph channel CTA before FAQs inviting watch and subscribe; correct YouTube link; metadata lengths and distinct title; entities actually used; no em or en dashes; no generic, repetitive, promotional or off-topic passages.
