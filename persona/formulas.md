# Formulas (from analysis of high-performing long-form explainer channels)

## Headline formulas (generate 5 variants per video, mix formulas)
1. Urgent future revelation: "The next [timeframe] of [topic]: why even [experts] are [strong emotion]"
2. Year sequence prediction: "[Year], [Year], [Year]: [bold statement about change]"
3. Authority pattern interrupt: "[Surprising statement]. What [insiders] aren't telling you about [hot topic]"
4. Specific number + consequence: "[Number] [things] that [consequence] by [date]"
5. Contrast: "[Common belief] is wrong. [What is actually happening]."

Rules: under 60 characters where possible, front-load the hook (mobile truncates at ~50), include a time element or a number, create tension without lying. No clickbait that the video does not pay off within the first ninety seconds.

## Five elements every strong title has at least three of
future prediction, emotional intensity (fear, excitement, awe), time-bound element, authority signal, unexpected contrast.

## Script sequence (long video)
1. Pattern-breaking opening (0:00-0:20): bold, unexpected, specific claim. State the stakes.
2. Authority (0:20-0:50): why this perspective matters: sources, data, insider framing. No personal credential claims the persona cannot back.
3. Framework reveal (0:50-1:30): "There are three stages to this" / "Two forces are pulling in opposite directions". Preview the structure.
4. Deep dive (1:30-9:00): deliver each part with specifics, surprising facts, contrasts, a short story or example per part. Callback to the framework at each transition.
5. Transformative close (last 60-90s): connect to the viewer's next twelve months. One emotionally resonant line. One question for the comments. One-sentence CTA.

## Engagement triggers
- A question to the audience inside the video, not only at the end.
- "Is this actually worth it" style debates where honest.
- Specific, named sources create trust; vague "experts say" does not.

## Psychological triggers (use honestly)
future glimpse, expert concern, transformation narrative, clear pattern, acceleration/urgency.

## Shorts formula
Line 1: the hook (a claim, a number, or a contradiction). Lines 2-6: one idea, one example, one consequence. Last line: a twist or a question. 110-150 words.

## Thumbnail text
Max three words. All caps. One emotional word allowed. Must make sense next to the title without repeating it.
