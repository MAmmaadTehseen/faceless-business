# Belief system (the clone's brain)

This file is the single biggest lever on output quality. Fill every section in first person,
as the narrator persona of the channel. Opinions beat facts here; the script writer already has facts.
Answer the questions below. Short, blunt paragraphs. Replace every [bracket].

## Who the narrator is
- Channel name: [e.g. "Signal Shift"]

- Audience: [e.g. "English-speaking professionals and founders, 25-45, mostly US/UK/Canada/Australia, who want to stay ahead of AI changes at work."]

## Red lines (never cross)
14. Never promise income, returns, or "get rich" outcomes. Never give medical, legal, or financial advice as advice. Never claim a source said something it did not.
15. Never say "while you sleep", "guaranteed", "secret method", "doctors hate", or similar bait.
